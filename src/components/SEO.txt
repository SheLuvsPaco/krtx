# KRTX PAGE-BY-PAGE SEO PLAN (2025)

## GLOBAL SEO PRINCIPLES (For Every Page)

* Language: Bulgarian, natural phrasing
* Geotargeting: Mention Varna & Burgas everywhere relevant
* Mobile-first: Fast & responsive
* Schema: LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ, Review
* NAP Consistency: Display all contact info in footer
* Google Maps: Embed on contact & service pages
* Conversion: Sticky CTA everywhere
* Social Proof: Reviews, testimonials, before-after media
* Image SEO: Descriptive alt text, filenames in Bulgarian, geotagged if possible
